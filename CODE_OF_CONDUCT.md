# Community conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Discuss the work, give specific feedback,
and accept that people have different experience levels. Chinese and English are
both welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information and spam
are not acceptable. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict
participation to keep the community safe. Report concerns through a private
maintainer contact or GitHub's reporting tools; do not publish another person's
private details.
