## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-28

Initial public source release of Yeyu, a Hermes-powered editable PPTX workspace.

- React and shadcn/ui workspace with per-slide conversations and photo placement.
- Native PPTX template/material reuse, tags, visual selection and five AI-ranked alternatives.
- Bounded parallel page workers, checkpoints, targeted repair and Office previews.
- User accounts, administrator-only material catalog and configurable generation quotas.
- Private encrypted model connections with streaming/tool-call connection tests.
- Document-derived chapter navigation, editable outline and consistent typography.
- Projects, folders, recycle-bin recovery and one contextual permanent-delete action.
- Installation, security, contribution and third-party attribution documentation.

Private user material libraries, logos, documents, accounts and generated decks
are intentionally not distributed.
